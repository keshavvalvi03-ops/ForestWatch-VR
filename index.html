📐 Mathematical & Ecological Formulations

The simulator uses the following illustrative mathematical models parameterized from IPCC Tier 1 tropical biomass averages:

  • Carbon Sequestration Rate:
    C_seq = N_trees × 0.022 tCO₂/year
    (Assumes standard tropical moist tree baseline of ~22 kg CO₂/tree/yr)
  • Soil Erosion & Runoff Risk Index:
    E_risk = min(100, max(5, 100 - (CanopyCover% × 0.92)))
    (Models loss of foliage rainfall kinetic energy dissipation)
  • Biodiversity Carrying Capacity Index:
    B_index = (CanopyCover% / 100)^1.25 × 100
    (Non-linear habitat islanding penalty representing fragmentation)
  • Canopy Microclimate Temperature Anomaly:
    ΔT = (1 - CanopyCover% / 100) × 3.4°C
    (Surface heating due to loss of latent heat evapotranspiration)

🔬 Scientific Integrity & Data Sources

To maintain academic rigor, the project explicitly separates real-world satellite ground-truth observations from educational virtual approximations:

1. Global Forest Watch / Hansen et al. (Science 2013, updated 2023):
High-resolution global maps of 21st-century forest cover change based on time-series analysis of Landsat imagery.
2. FAO Global Forest Resources Assessment (FRA 2020):
United Nations Food and Agriculture Organization official forestry biomass metrics and regional loss tables.
3. NASA Earth Observatory & Landsat Surface Reflectance:
Documented time-series telemetry of Brazilian Amazon BR-364 and Borneo peatland drainage.
4. W3C WebXR Device API Specification:
Web standards for accessing virtual reality and augmented reality devices in the browser.
⚠️
Scientific & Academic Disclaimer: The 3D tree counts and ecological indices presented in the simulator are illustrative models designed for environmental education and spatial visualization. Virtual planting does not replace real-world ecological reforestation, and calculated metrics should not be interpreted as physical survey results without ground-truth calibration.
